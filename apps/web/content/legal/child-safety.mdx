These standards apply to the **Skedaddle** app, published by **Skedaddle Enterprises Inc.**

## Our standard

Skedaddle has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse
material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion and any content or conduct that sexualizes minors. Any such content or
behavior is prohibited and results in immediate account removal and reporting to the authorities.

## Who can use Skedaddle

Skedaddle is for adults aged 18 and over. Family features let parents describe their children's age ranges to
find other families, but children do not have accounts and no child's name, photo or contact details are collected
as part of a family profile.

## How to report

- **In the app:** every profile, chat message, post and story has a **Report** option,
  and you can **Block** any user at any time.
- **By email:** [support@skedaddleenterprises26.com](mailto:support@skedaddleenterprises26.com?subject=Child%20safety%20report)
  with the subject "Child safety report".
- If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.

## What we do with reports

- Reports related to child safety are reviewed by our team as a priority.
- Content that violates these standards is removed and the account responsible is banned.
- Apparent CSAM is reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to law
  enforcement as required by law, and preserved as the law requires.
- We cooperate with law enforcement requests related to child safety.

## Compliance

We comply with applicable child safety laws, including reporting obligations for CSAM under U.S. federal law.

## Child safety contact

Our designated point of contact for child safety matters is reachable at
[support@skedaddleenterprises26.com](mailto:support@skedaddleenterprises26.com).
