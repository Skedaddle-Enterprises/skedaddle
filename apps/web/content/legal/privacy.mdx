## Overview

This Privacy Policy describes what information Skedaddle Enterprises, Inc.
("Skedaddle," "we," "us") collects when you visit this website or join our
waitlist, and how we use, share, and protect it.

## Information we collect

- **Waitlist signups.** If you submit your email through a waitlist form, we
  store your email address, the page you signed up from, a timestamp, and
  basic technical details (such as your browser's user agent string).
- **Site analytics.** We log anonymous pageview events: the page visited,
  the referring page, a randomly generated visitor id stored in your
  browser, and a coarse, city/country-level location resolved from your IP
  address via a third-party geolocation lookup. We do not store your full
  IP address ourselves.

We do not collect payment information or precise GPS location through
this website. The Skedaddle app also offers an optional ID verification
feature, described below, that involves a government-issued ID image.

## How we use information

We use waitlist emails to notify you about Skedaddle's launch and product
updates. We use pageview analytics, in aggregate, to understand which pages
are visited and roughly where from, so we can improve the site. We do not
use your information for automated decision-making that produces legal or
similarly significant effects.

## How long we keep information

We retain waitlist signups until you ask us to delete them (see Your
choices, below) or until a reasonable period after the product has
launched and the waitlist has served its purpose. Pageview analytics
records are retained in aggregate for site-improvement purposes.

## Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information associated
with your email address at any time by contacting us through the
[support page](/support). We will honor deletion requests for waitlist
data unless we are required to keep it for a legitimate business or legal
reason.

## Children's privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not
knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Third parties

We use Firebase (Google Cloud) to store waitlist and analytics data, and a
third-party IP geolocation API to resolve coarse location for analytics.
These providers process data on our behalf and under their own security
and privacy commitments. We do not sell or rent your personal information
to third parties.

## Security

We use Firebase's built-in access controls and security rules to restrict
who can read or write waitlist and analytics data. No method of
transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to
protect your information.

## Skedaddle iOS app

The Skedaddle iOS app collects the following, in addition to the website
data described above:

- **Account information.** Name, email address, and profile details you
  provide when creating an account.
- **Location.** With your permission, we collect precise or coarse device
  location to help you find and join nearby meetups. You can disable
  location access at any time in iOS Settings.
- **Photos and camera.** With your permission, we access your photo
  library or camera when you choose to upload a profile photo or event
  image.
- **Push notification tokens.** If you enable notifications, we store
  your device's push token (via Apple Push Notification service) to send
  you updates about events and messages.
- **Device and usage data.** Device model, OS version, app version, crash
  logs, and basic in-app analytics (screens viewed, features used).

The app requests iOS permissions (Location, Photos/Camera, Notifications)
only when needed for the corresponding feature. Denying a permission may
limit related functionality but will not block use of the rest of the
app. We use Firebase (Google Cloud) for authentication, data storage, and
push notification delivery, and Apple's Push Notification service to
deliver notifications.

## Skedaddle Android app

The Skedaddle Android app collects the following, in addition to the
website data described above:

- **Account information.** Name, email address, and profile details you
  provide when creating an account.
- **Location.** With your permission, we collect precise or coarse device
  location to help you find and join nearby meetups. You can disable
  location access at any time in Android Settings.
- **Photos and media.** With your permission, we access your device
  storage or camera when you choose to upload a profile photo or event
  image.
- **Push notification tokens.** If you enable notifications, we store
  your device's push token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) to send you
  updates about events and messages.
- **Device and usage data.** Device model, OS version, app version, crash
  logs, and basic in-app analytics (screens viewed, features used).

The app requests Android permissions (Location, Storage/Camera,
Notifications) only when needed for the corresponding feature. Denying a
permission may limit related functionality but will not block use of the
rest of the app. We use Firebase (Google Cloud) for authentication, data
storage, and push notification delivery via Firebase Cloud Messaging.

## ID verification

If you choose to verify your identity, you may submit a photo of a
government-issued ID (such as a driver's license or passport) along with
a selfie for comparison. This is entirely optional and unlocks a
"Verified" badge on your profile.

- Your ID image and selfie are reviewed solely to confirm your identity
  and that you meet our minimum age requirement.
- They are transmitted over encrypted connections, stored in a
  restricted-access environment, and are permanently deleted within 30
  days of submission regardless of the outcome.
- They are never shared with other users, used for profiling, or sold to
  third parties.
- Opting out of ID verification has no effect on your access to the app's
  core features.
