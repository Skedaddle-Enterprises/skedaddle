This Privacy Policy explains what information Skedaddle Enterprises, Inc. ("Skedaddle", "we", "us") collects through the Skedaddle apps for iOS and Android and through this website, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal information.

## Information you give us

- **Account details.** Your name, username, email address, mobile number, city and state, and your password (stored securely by Firebase Authentication; we never see it). If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email from them.
- **Your setup answers.** What you tell us during setup and in your profile, such as who's joining you, your interests, energy level, availability, preferred group size, and travel radius. If you use Family features, this can include your children's age ranges. We never ask for children's names or photos.
- **Student verification.** If you verify as a student: your school email, university, graduation year, and, if you ask for manual review, your name, phone number, and a selfie (iOS). Automatic checks are done by our partner VerifyPass.
- **Content you post.** Footprints posts and stories, photos you choose to upload, reactions, and messages you send in Tribe, Family, and Student chats.
- **Messages to Scout.** The questions you ask our AI planner, Scout.
- **Reports and blocks.** When you report content or block someone, we store who reported or blocked whom, what was reported, and why.

## Information collected with your permission

- **Location.** With your permission, the app uses your device location to show nearby events, places, and weather. Location is sent to our servers and partners only to run those searches, and it is not stored on your profile. Your profile keeps only the city you entered. You can turn off location access at any time in your device settings.
- **Photos.** We access only the photos you pick to post. We don't scan your photo library.
- **Calendar (iOS).** If you allow it, the app adds plans you confirm to your calendar. It does not read your existing events.

## Information collected automatically

- **Security checks.** We use Firebase App Check (Apple App Attest / Google Play Integrity) to confirm requests come from the real app. This uses device attestation tokens, not your personal information.
- **Purchases.** If you subscribe, Apple or Google handles the payment. We receive your subscription status, not your card details. On Android we use RevenueCat to manage subscription status.
- **Website analytics.** On this website we log anonymous page views: the page, the referring page, a random visitor id stored in your browser, and a city or country-level location worked out from your IP address. We do not store your full IP address.

The apps do **not** use advertising trackers, do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and do not show ads.

## How we use information

- To create and secure your account and run the app.
- To match you with people in your city for Tribes, Family Pods, and Student Pods. Matching uses your setup answers and your city.
- To show events, places, and plans near you.
- To verify student status and unlock student features.
- To answer your questions in Scout.
- To keep the community safe: review reports, remove content, and suspend abusive accounts.
- To send service emails, such as password resets and verification results.
- To manage subscriptions.

## Who we share information with

**With other users:** your name, profile initial, city, and the posts and messages you choose to share are visible to the people you're matched or chatting with, and Footprints posts are visible to other Skedaddle users. Your email, phone number, and student email are never shown to other users.

**With service providers** who process data for us under contract, only as needed:

- **Google Firebase / Google Cloud**: accounts, database, file storage, and our servers.
- **Apple and Google**: Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, and in-app purchases.
- **VerifyPass**: student status checks.
- **Groq**: processes your Scout messages to generate replies. On iPhone, some AI features run entirely on your device using Apple Intelligence and never leave it.
- **Yelp, Ticketmaster, and Open-Meteo**: receive a location and search terms (never your identity) to return nearby places, events, and weather.
- **SendGrid**: sends our service emails.
- **RevenueCat** (Android): subscription status.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users or the public.

## How long we keep information

- **Account and profile data:** until you delete your account.
- **Verification selfies:** deleted automatically 90 days after submission.
- **Footprints stories:** disappear after 12 hours.
- **Reports:** kept as long as needed to keep the community safe and meet legal obligations.
- **Website waitlist emails:** until you ask us to delete them.

## Deleting your account and your choices

You can delete your account at any time in the app: **Profile → Delete Account**. This deletes your profile, matching data, and student verification records. You can also see [how to delete your account](/delete-account) or ask us to access, correct, or delete your information by emailing [support@skedaddleenterprises26.com](mailto:support@skedaddleenterprises26.com).

You can turn off location, photo, and calendar access at any time in your device settings.

## Children's privacy

Skedaddle is not intended for children under 13, and children cannot create accounts. Parents using Family features may share only their children's age ranges, never names or photos. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it. See our [Child Safety Standards](/child-safety).

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is restricted by Firebase security rules, and our API keys stay on our servers rather than in the apps. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU, or the UK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or move your data, and to object to certain processing. Email us to use these rights; we will respond within the time the law requires. We do not sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If the changes are significant, we will tell you in the app or by email. The date above shows the latest update.
