## Where we are today

Skedaddle is pre-launch. The Tribe-matching product described elsewhere on
this site does not exist yet as a live service — this page describes our
intent and current state honestly, rather than promising features that
aren't built.

## What Skedaddle does not yet implement

To be direct about current limitations:

- Skedaddle does not yet implement identity or background verification for
  waitlist signups.
- Skedaddle does not yet have an in-product reporting or moderation system,
  because there is no live in-app experience yet.
- Skedaddle does not yet have a dedicated Trust & Safety team headcount
  beyond early founding roles.
- Skedaddle does not yet publish a formal incident-response or abuse
  policy.

## What we intend to build

Before any real-world Tribe matching goes live, we intend to put in place:
identity checks appropriate to in-person meetups, a way to report a concern
about another member, and a clear escalation path for safety issues. These
are commitments for the product roadmap, not yet-shipped features.

## Reporting a concern today

If you have a trust or safety concern about this website or the waitlist
(for example, suspected abuse of the signup form), please reach out via the
[support page](/support).
