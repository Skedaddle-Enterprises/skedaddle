## Getting help

Skedaddle is pre-launch, so there is no in-app support experience yet. If
you have a question about the waitlist, this website, or Skedaddle's plans,
this page describes how support will work.

## What we can help with today

- Questions about how the waitlist works and what happens after you sign up
- Corrections or removal requests for information you submitted (e.g. your
  waitlist email)
- Reporting a bug or issue with this website

## What we can't help with yet

Because the matching product hasn't launched, we can't yet help with
in-Tribe issues, scheduling, or account recovery — those flows don't exist
yet.
