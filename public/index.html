The platform

Four layers.
One operating system for belonging.

01

Tribes individual layer

Small, AI-matched cohorts of 5–7 people. Matched on availability, interests, life stage, personality, and geography. The system proposes weekly shared experiences and tracks connection strength over time. Strangers become real friends through repeated contact.

Strangers → real friends
02

Family Pods household layer

Families are multi-person systems with competing schedules and kid-specific constraints. Skedaddle coordinates internal family experiences and cross-matches families based on children's ages, parenting values, and activity preferences. Highest LTV segment in the platform.

Highest retention tier
03

Community OS institution layer

Churches, schools, and homeschool co-ops get a full belonging infrastructure. New members get household intake, interest capture, and automated matching to small groups, ministry roles, and activities. Admins track newcomer integration scores in real time.

SaaS revenue engine
04

Connection Graph™ intelligence layer

A behavioral property graph capturing every real-world experience outcome — who attended, satisfaction scores, repeat contact, trust signals, and retention effects. It learns what creates belonging and makes every future coordination smarter. This is the defensible moat.

Experience flow

From sign-up
to real friendship.

The coordination engine handles everything from intake to outcome. You just show up.

1

Profile & intake

Life stage, interests, schedule, social comfort level, and personality style — captured once, refined continuously through behavior.

2

Tribe assignment

The Assimilation Engine matches you to 5–7 compatible people nearby with overlapping availability and shared core interests.

3

AI proposes the experience

Weather check, venue selection, travel time fairness, RSVP flow — all handled automatically. You get a ready-to-confirm invite.
