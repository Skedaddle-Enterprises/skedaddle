The platform

Four layers.
One operating system for belonging.

01

Tribes individual layer

Small, AI-matched cohorts of 5–7 people. Matched on availability, interests, life stage, personality, and geography. The system proposes weekly shared experiences and tracks connection strength over time.

Strangers → real friends
02

Family Pods household layer

Families are multi-person systems with competing schedules. Skedaddle coordinates internal family experiences and cross-matches families based on children's ages, parenting values, and activity preferences.

Highest retention tier
03

Community OS institution layer

Churches, schools, and homeschool co-ops get a full belonging infrastructure. New members get automated matching to small groups, ministry roles, and activities. Admins track integration scores in real time.

SaaS revenue engine
04

Connection Graph™ intelligence layer

A behavioral property graph capturing every real-world experience outcome — attendance, satisfaction, repeat contact, trust signals. It learns what creates belonging and makes every future coordination smarter.

Experience flow

From sign-up
to real friendship.

The coordination engine handles everything from intake to outcome. You just show up.

1

Profile & intake

Life stage, interests, schedule, social comfort level, and personality style — captured once, refined continuously through behavior.

2

Tribe assignment

The Assimilation Engine matches you to 5–7 compatible people nearby with overlapping availability and shared core interests.

3

AI proposes the experience

Weather check, venue selection, travel time fairness, RSVP flow — all handled automatically. You get a ready-to-confirm invite.

4

Show up and connect

Post-experience feedback feeds back into the Connection Graph, improving every future match for you and your tribe.
