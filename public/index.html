Experience flow

From sign-up
to real friendship.

The coordination engine handles everything from intake to outcome. You just show up.

1

Profile & intake

Life stage, interests, schedule, social comfort level, and personality style — captured once, refined continuously through behavior.

2

Tribe assignment

The Assimilation Engine matches you to 5–7 compatible people nearby with overlapping availability and shared core interests.

3

AI proposes the experience

Weather check, venue selection, travel time fairness, RSVP flow — all handled automatically. You get a ready-to-confirm invite.

4

Show up and connect

Post-experience feedback feeds back into the Connection Graph, improving every future match for you and your tribe.
